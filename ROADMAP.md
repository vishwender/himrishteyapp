## Dependencies and decision gates

- First implementation candidate is T0.1: JavaScript starter alignment, after review. It does not depend on backend availability. T0.2 establishes the runnable baseline before introducing native dependencies.
- Contract collection T0.3 can proceed independently of starter cleanup. Never block local presentation unnecessarily, but keep fixtures explicit and leave live integration unchecked.
- Authentication mechanism determines storage, transport and restore architecture. Do not install an auth/storage stack before this decision is grounded in the backend.
- Design acceptance is required before broad screen rollout. Core flows take precedence over decorative polish.
- Profiles supply member identity/presentation for discover and relationship actions. Membership rules can affect phases 3–5; capture entitlement semantics during phase 0 even though purchase UI comes later.
- In-app feed/settings may proceed before membership if they do not reference unfinished destinations. Push and payments require separate external setup and staging/sandbox evidence.
- A failed gate remains open with evidence and a next action. No dates or estimates are promised before baseline and contract uncertainty are resolved.

Each phase should ship in small reviewable increments from TASKS.md. Completion requires its acceptance criteria and verification evidence, not merely a screen or mock implementation.
